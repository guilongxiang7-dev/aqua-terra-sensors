AQUA / TERRA — FIRMWARE NOTES
Guilong Xiang

WATER SYSTEM
Nano-compatible ATmega328P controller.
DHT11 data D2; DS18B20 data D3 with 4.7 kOhm pull-up; TDS A0; optical voltage A1; serial pH D8/D9; OLED SDA A4, SCL A5.
Dependencies: Arduino core, Wire, SoftwareSerial, U8g2, DHT sensor library, OneWire, DallasTemperature. Some DHT library installations also need Adafruit Unified Sensor.
The archived pH sketch tries both D8/D9 serial orientations at 9600 baud. Verify the module pin labels and electrical requirements against its own documentation.
The TDS routine takes 30 ADC samples, uses median filtering and temperature compensation, and caps its numeric output at 1400 ppm. The original display prints 1400, without an explicit >= marker. The website uses the report's >=1400 notation for the lemon-water record; this is a censored lower bound, not an exact concentration.
Optical sensor output remains voltage. It is not calibrated turbidity in NTU.

SOIL SYSTEM
Nano-compatible ATmega328P; SN-3001-TR four-in-one probe; automatic-direction TTL-RS485 converter; SSD1306 OLED.
Converter TXD to D8 (controller RX), RXD to D9 (controller TX); probe A/B to converter A/B; OLED SDA A4, SCL A5.
Modbus RTU: 4800 baud, address 1, function 0x03, registers 0x0000–0x0003. Moisture 0.1%, signed temperature 0.1 degC, EC 1 microS/cm, pH 0.1 unit. The programme validates CRC-16 and filters changes before display.
Dependencies: Arduino core, Wire, SoftwareSerial, U8g2. Check the actual probe supply rating. The documented project wiring uses 5 V; this does not establish the supply requirements of another probe sold under a similar name.

DATA PROVENANCE AND INTERPRETATION
Primary values: final report Tables 6, 9, 11 and 12. Statistics: Tables 7 and 10.
Lemon-water optical voltage: report Table 12 = 3.20 V; final poster Table 4 = 4.20 V. The website follows the report and exposes this discrepancy.
The report's TDS analysis includes five pairs, including the salt-water reading of 1400 ppm at the display ceiling. Lemon water is excluded. The boundary reading should not be treated as conclusively uncensored.
Printed material rows total CNY 0.01 less than the report's combined totals. Original reported totals are retained.
The sample set is small. A non-significant paired t-test does not prove equivalence or performance outside the tested samples.
